Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Melody's Morels, Clay County, Missouri

1. Who We Are

Melody's Morels is a morel mushroom hunting app operated from Clay County, Missouri. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website, mobile application, or services.

2. Information We Collect

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google.

Find reports: When you submit a morel sighting, we collect the information you provide: location name, GPS coordinates (if you choose to share them), date, quantity, size, notes, and any photos you upload.

Land listings: Landowners provide property location, description, pricing, and photos. Exact addresses are stored securely and only shared with confirmed, paid bookers.

Payment information: Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your credit card number. We receive transaction records including amounts and dates.

Location data: If you use the GPS feature, your device's location is used to auto-fill location fields. We do not track your location continuously.

Usage data: We collect standard usage logs (pages visited, features used, device type) to improve the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the App and its features
  • To process bookings and payments
  • To send push notifications about morel activity in your selected regions (with your permission)
  • To display your find reports in the community feed
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • To prevent fraud and enforce our Terms of Service

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as follows:

  • Landowners and hunters: After a confirmed booking, the hunter receives the exact property address and the landowner receives the hunter's name and email
  • Stripe: Payment processing — governed by Stripe's Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google): Database, authentication, and file storage — governed by Google's Privacy Policy
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users

5. Photos and User Content

Photos you upload are stored in Firebase Storage and displayed publicly in the community feed. You can delete your posts at any time from your account.

6. Push Notifications

Push notifications are optional. You can enable them by clicking the 🔔 bell icon and granting permission. You can disable notifications at any time through your device or browser settings.

7. Advertising

Free users may see ads served by Google AdSense (web) or Google AdMob (mobile app). These services may use cookies and device identifiers to show relevant ads. Premium subscribers do not see ads. See Google's advertising policies for more information.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account information as long as your account is active. Find reports and community content are retained until you delete them or close your account. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

Melody's Morels is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

We use Firebase's industry-standard security infrastructure to protect your data. Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth with encrypted credentials. Payment data is handled by Stripe and never stored on our servers.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time
  • Export your find reports

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes by email or in-app notification.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Visit our Contact page or email us through the inquiry form on the app.
